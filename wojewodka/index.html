01 Context

My first freelance project

The client found my profile on Behance and reached out directly — an established family law firm in Poland that wanted a simple, modern refresh of their website.

I worked closely with the youngest member of the family, who wanted to bring a fresh touch to the site and add some of his own graphics. Using Figma, I designed a clean, updated look — one that balanced the firm's long-standing history with a more modern feel.
