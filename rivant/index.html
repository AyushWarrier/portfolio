The agency's own website

During my internship at RIVANT Media, I designed their new agency website — a place to show the work clearly, with a little motion to make it feel alive.

The goal was simple: present the services well, and add subtle animation without letting it get in the way. I designed the whole thing in Figma.

The Build

Designing for handoff

To make the developers' job easier, I was recommended to design against a component library. After trying a few, Aceternity UI fit RIVANT's site best — designing with pre-built elements meant the team could build faster, with less back-and-forth about intent.

I worked closely with the developers through the build, checking the implemented animations against the design so the final site matched what was drawn in Figma.
